The Genesis

A Vision Born from Obsession

In 1987, Arthur Morgan stood in front of a modest showroom in New Cairo with just three cars and an audacious dream. He wasn't a businessman by trade—he was a dreamer, an engineer, and an uncompromising perfectionist who believed that automobiles were more than machines; they were mobile art forms.

Arthur had spent over a decade in Europe, learning from the greatest craftspeople in automotive history. He studied under Ferrari master craftsmen, trained alongside Porsche engineers, and formed relationships with the families who built these legendary machines. That invaluable experience became the foundation of APEX MOTORS.

"I didn't want to sell cars. I wanted to curate experiences for people who understood that true luxury isn't about quantity—it's about the stories each vehicle carries with it."
— Arthur Morgan, Founder

The first year was brutal. Banks rejected his loan applications. Suppliers questioned his vision. But Arthur persisted, funding operations through personal savings and demonstrating an unparalleled commitment to quality that eventually caught the attention of European manufacturers.

Our Journey

The Evolution of Excellence

1987
The Beginning
Started with 3 vehicles: a 1963 Ferrari 250 GTO, a Lamborghini Miura, and a Porsche 911 Carrera RS. Focus was pure: authenticity and provenance above all else.
1991
First Major Partnership
Established direct partnerships with Ferrari factory. Became the trusted source for verified, low-mileage examples. Built reputation through meticulous documentation practices.
1998
International Recognition
Expanded to handle ultra-rare vehicles: Rolls-Royce, Bentley, and Aston Martin acquisitions. Started consulting with collectors worldwide. Implemented the proprietary 217-point inspection process.
2007
Crisis & Evolution
During financial crisis, APEX thrived by focusing on genuine discretionary luxury. While others struggled, our client base expanded due to reputation for guaranteed authenticity and stability.
2015
Digital Transformation
Introduced AI-powered vehicle authentication. Invested in advanced restoration facilities. Pioneered virtual viewing experience for international clients preparing acquisitions.
2020-2026
Modern Luxury
Integrated hybrid and electric supercars. Expanded bespoke customization. Established heritage restoration division. Now serving 4th-generation family collectors while maintaining founding principles.

Defining Moments

Stories That Shaped Us

The 250 GTO Recovery
In 1992, Marcus tracked down a stolen 1962 Ferrari 250 GTO for three years and successfully recovered it for its original family. This defining moment established our reputation for ethical operation and passion for automotive history.
Global Collector Network
Over three decades, we've established personal relationships with private collectors across six continents. These relationships have given us first access to vehicles that never reach public markets—vehicles with extraordinary provenance.
Legacy Preservation
We preserve automotive heritage through our museum-quality restoration facility. Vehicles that arrive damaged, neglected, or in pieces are restored to original specification with complete documentation of the process.

What Drives Us

Our Operating Philosophy
